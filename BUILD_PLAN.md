# iOSotorola Build Plan

## Goal
Investigate whether iOS 16.5 can run on the Motorola Moto G(60) (XT2135-2).

## Device
- Model: Motorola Moto G(60)
- Model number: XT2135-2
- RAM: 6 GB
- Android device codename: hanoip (needs verification)

## Development stages
1. Verify the exact device model and hardware.
2. Research the bootloader, kernel, and device-tree support.
3. Study the HackiOS project and its hardware requirements.
4. Investigate feasibility without modifying the phone.
5. Develop and test only if a compatible, lawful approach is established.

## Safety
- Do not flash unverified images.
- Do not modify boot partitions during initial research.
- Back up important data before any future device modifications.
- Do not include proprietary Apple firmware files in this repository.

## Current status
Research and planning. No bootable iOS image has been created.
